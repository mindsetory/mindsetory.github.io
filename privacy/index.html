Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Mindsetory (we) operates a private publishing tool used only by Mindsetory to schedule and publish its own content (videos and posts) to its own social media accounts, including TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube. This tool is not offered to the public.

What we access: when Mindsetory connects its TikTok account, we receive only the basic profile information of that account (display name and avatar) and an access token that lets the tool upload or publish videos that Mindsetory itself created.

How we use it: the data is used only to publish Mindsetory's own content to Mindsetory's own account. We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Storage: access tokens are stored encrypted on a private server controlled by Mindsetory and are never shown to anyone else.

Retention and deletion: Mindsetory can disconnect the account at any time from the tool, which deletes the stored token, and can also revoke access from TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Apps and services).
